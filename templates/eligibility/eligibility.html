How Company-Specific Eligibility Verification Works

✓ No Single Global Rule: Each employer sets independent criteria stored across COMPANY, COMPANY_BRANCH, and COMPANY_SKILL relational tables.

✓ 6 Criteria Evaluated:

  • CGPA: Candidate CGPA ≥ Company Minimum CGPA
  • 10th Board %: Candidate 10th Score ≥ Company Minimum 10th %
  • 12th Board %: Candidate 12th Score ≥ Company Minimum 12th %
  • Backlogs: Candidate Active Backlogs ≤ Company Maximum Allowed
  • Branch: Candidate Department must match one of the company's allowed branches
  • Required Skills: Candidate must possess all technical skills specified by the employer

✓ Comprehensive Audit: All criteria are evaluated unconditionally. If any criterion fails, the complete list of unmet requirements is clearly displayed.
